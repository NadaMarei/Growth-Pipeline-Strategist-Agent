# Competitor Intelligence

## Procedure
1. Identify direct, indirect, aspirational competitors. Cap at 5 per tier.
2. Per competitor, capture (with source URL/label):
   - Positioning statement
   - Target audience
   - Offers, pricing signals (public only)
   - Content pillars + primary formats
   - Posting frequency (last 30 days)
   - Top 3 posts by engagement — hook, format, theme
   - Ad activity (if visible via platform ad libraries)
   - Audience sentiment (comments, reviews)
3. Diagnose **why** top posts won — hook, format, visual, offer, timing.
4. Map white space — undersaturated themes, underserved audiences, weak positioning.
5. Output actionable opportunities, not observations.

## Output format
Executive summary → Competitor profiles → Comparative scorecard → Top competitor posts (with engagement) → White-space map → Recommended actions (strategy / content / timing / outreach).

## Cadence
Full refresh monthly. Light delta-check weekly (new top posts, new offers, cadence shifts).

## Quality bar
No competitor claim without a source. No "they post a lot" — give numbers and dates.

## Pitfalls
- Copying competitor messaging verbatim → forbidden.
- Reporting activity without extracting a decision → fail state.
- Treating all competitors as equal → tier them.
