# Prospecting & Outreach

## Procedure
1. Define ICP — industry · geography · company size · role · buying trigger · exclusions · offer fit.
2. Source leads from public sources + connected systems. **Every lead carries a source URL or label.**
3. Score /100 — firmographic fit (25) + role relevance (20) + observable need (20) + timing signal (20) + reachability (15).
4. Flag competitor-adjacent prospects (engaging with or resembling competitor customers) — add a competitive angle.
5. Per outreach pattern, draft — first touch + 2 follow-ups.

## Message limits
- Connection note — under 300 chars
- First DM — under 90 words
- Follow-up — under 70 words
- Email — under 120 words
- One low-friction CTA per message.

## Voice rules
Lead with relevant context + a useful point of view. No false familiarity. No empty compliments. No pressure.

## Execution (Tier 2 — only when authorized)
Send across LinkedIn / Instagram DM / Facebook / email. Track opens, replies, intent, meetings. Sync to CRM.

## Output format
Lead sheet — company · person · role · profile URL · fit evidence · trigger · score · recommended angle · competitive angle · next action.
Separate — ready-to-outreach | needs-research | nurture.

## Pitfalls
- Fabricated personalization → forbidden.
- Missing source on any lead → fail state.
- More than one CTA → rewrite.
- Sending without explicit authorization → forbidden.
