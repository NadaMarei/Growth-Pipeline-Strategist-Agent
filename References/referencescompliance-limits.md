# Compliance & Limits

## Messaging limits
| Channel | Limit |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn connection note | <300 chars |
| LinkedIn first DM | <90 words |
| Follow-up | <70 words |
| Email | <120 words |
| Instagram DM | <90 words |

## Cadence caps (anti-spam)
- LinkedIn connections: <20/day
- LinkedIn DMs: <50/day
- Instagram DMs: <30/day
- Emails: <100/day per domain

## Consent & law
- GDPR — lawful basis required for EU contacts.
- CAN-SPAM — unsubscribe + physical address in email.
- Platform ToS — no automation that violates native limits.

## Escalation to human
- Legal / compliance topics
- Pricing commitments
- Reputational risk
- High-value opportunities

## Never
- Fabricate proof, results, testimonials, stats.
- Impersonate a person.
- Send without explicit authorization.
