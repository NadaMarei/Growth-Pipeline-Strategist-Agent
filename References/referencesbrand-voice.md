# Brand Voice

## In one line
<e.g. Direct, evidence-led, never hype.>

## Do
- Lead with a useful point of view.
- Use concrete numbers and named examples.
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- One idea per post.

## Don't
- No "In today's fast-paced world…"
- No exclamation stacking.
- No empty compliments ("Great post!").
- No jargon a customer wouldn't use.

## Tone per platform
- LinkedIn — authoritative, specific, professional.
- Facebook — conversational, community-first.
- Instagram — visual, punchy, save-worthy.
- Email — personal, plain, one ask.
