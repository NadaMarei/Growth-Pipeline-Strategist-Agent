# Platform Playbook

## LinkedIn
- Best formats: text, document carousel, single image, native video.
- Ideal length: 120–250 words.
- Cadence: 4–5 / week.
- Best times: Tue–Thu, 07:00–09:00 local.
- Comments matter more than likes for reach.

## Facebook
- Best formats: link post with context, image, discussion prompt, short video.
- Ideal length: 80–220 words.
- Cadence: 3–5 / week.
- Groups > Page for community reach.

## Instagram
- Best formats: Reels, carousels, single image with strong caption.
- Caption: 80–180 words.
- Hashtags: 3–8 relevant.
- Saves + shares > likes.
- DM intent is the highest-value signal.
