# Hook Library

## Post hooks that earn the second line
- "Most [audience] get [problem] wrong. Here's what actually works."
- "[Number] [audience] told us [insight]. We tested it."
- "We lost [X] because of [mistake]. Don't repeat it."
- "[Common belief] is costing you [cost]. Proof:"
- "[Specific result] in [timeframe]. The playbook:"

## DM first-touch openers
- "Saw your post on [topic]. Quick question —"
- "You're hiring for [role] — usually means [trigger]. Worth a look?"
- "[Mutual contact] suggested we talk about [problem]."

## CTA patterns (pick one per post)
- "Want the template? Comment 'TEMPLATE'."
- "Reply with your biggest [X] challenge."
- "DM 'AUDIT' and I'll send a 3-point teardown."
- "Book a 15-min call — link in bio."

## Anti-patterns
- "In today's world…"
- "Let that sink in."
- "Agree?"
- "Thoughts?"
