# Winning Post Examples

## Example 1 — LinkedIn (authority + proof)
**Pillar:** Authority · **Stage:** Intent · **Format:** text

> Most B2B teams spend 6 months building a pipeline engine.
> We did it in 4 weeks — here's the exact stack:
> 1. …
> 2. …
> 3. …
> The unlock wasn't more tools. It was fixing the handoff.
> Full teardown in comments.

Why it worked: specific timeframe, numbered list, curiosity gap, comment CTA.

## Example 2 — Instagram (save-worthy carousel)
**Pillar:** Education · **Stage:** Engagement · **Format:** 8-slide carousel

Slide 1 (hook): "5 signs your LinkedIn strategy is broken"
Slide 2–6: one sign per slide, ≤20 words
Slide 7: recap
Slide 8 (CTA): "Save this. Send to your CMO."

Why it worked: high save rate, clear sequence, low-friction CTA.

## Example 3 — Facebook (discussion)
**Pillar:** Community · **Stage:** Engagement · **Format:** text

> Question for [audience]:
> What's the #1 thing you'd fix in your [process]?
> We're collecting answers for a benchmark report.

Why it worked: direct question, low effort to reply, invites discussion.
